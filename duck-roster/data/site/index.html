Install it now

Roster Sync is waiting for its Chrome Web Store listing to be approved. Until then you can add it by hand in Chrome or Edge on a computer. It takes about two minutes, and it's the same extension the store will have.

Chrome or Edge

  1. Download roster-sync.zip, then right-click it and choose Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents: the browser keeps using it.
  2. Type chrome://extensions into the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder, the one that contains manifest.json.
  4. Open your roster in Employee Online and click Connect Google Calendar on the panel at the bottom right.

Good to know

  1. Chrome may remind you that developer-mode extensions are on. That's expected for a hand-installed extension; leave it running.
  2. Hand-installed copies don't update themselves. Once the store version is out, remove this one and add the store version; your Work roster calendar stays as it is.
  3. Work computers often block Developer mode. If yours does, use your own computer: your shifts still reach your phone through the calendar.
  4. Firefox will get its own version from Firefox Add-ons.

How it works

Three steps, and you only do them once.

  1. 1

    Add to your browser

    Install Roster Sync from the Chrome, Edge or Firefox add-on store in one click. It's free.

  2. 2

    Open your roster in Employee Online

    Sign in the way you always do and open the month view. A small Sync panel appears at the bottom right.

  3. 3

    Connect your calendar once

    Click Connect Google Calendar on the Sync panel and approve. That's all the setup there is.

Then it just keeps up

Every time you open your roster, Roster Sync checks it against your calendar by itself. It only ever touches its own Work roster calendar, and days off are skipped.

  • New shifts are added
  • Changed shifts are updated
  • Removed shifts are deleted

Works with the calendar you already use

Roster Sync keeps a Work roster calendar in your Google account. Your phone, watch, Apple Calendar or Outlook then show it like any other calendar.

  • Google Calendar

    Updates within seconds

    The direct sync. Connect once from the Sync panel. A free Google account is all you need, even one you only use for this.

  • Apple Calendar

    Updates by itself

    iPhone, iPad and Mac. Add your Google account to Apple Calendar once and Work roster appears next to your other calendars.

  • Outlook

    Updates by itself

    Outlook on the web and Outlook.com can show your Google calendar too, at work or at home. Steps below.

  • Anything else

    One-off file

    Click the Roster Sync toolbar button and download the month as an .ics file. It's a snapshot, so it won't update by itself.

Set up iPhone, iPad or Mac

  1. iPhone or iPad: open Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Google (older iOS: Settings → Calendar → Accounts).
  2. Mac: open Calendar → Settings → Accounts, click + and choose Google.
  3. Sign in with the Google account you connected, and leave Calendars switched on.
  4. No Work roster? Tick it at google.com/calendar/syncselect.

Set up Outlook

  1. Outlook on the web or Outlook.com: open the calendar and choose Add calendar → Add personal calendars → Google. This updates by itself.
  2. If that option is missing: in Google Calendar open Settings → Work roster → Integrate calendar and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  3. In Outlook choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web and paste it. Outlook checks it every few hours.

Keep the secret address to yourself. Anyone who has it can see your shifts. If it ever gets out, click Reset next to it in Google Calendar. Roster Sync never sees your Apple or Microsoft account.

Privacy, in plain words

Your roster is your business. Roster Sync is built to see as little as it can.

Read the privacy policy
  • No password is ever asked for or stored.
  • It only reads the roster page you have open.
  • It can only see and change the calendar it created, never your other calendars.
  • Your shifts go straight from your browser to your Google Calendar. There is no Roster Sync server in between.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Common questions

Is this an official NSW Health app?

No. Roster Sync is an independent project made by a coworker. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NSW Health, NSW Health Pathology, Allocate or RLDatix.

Does it work on my phone?

Install it on a computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Your phone and watch then get your shifts through their calendar app, with nothing extra to install on them.

Do I need a Google account?

Yes. Roster Sync keeps your shifts in Google Calendar, and Apple Calendar and Outlook pick them up from there. A free Google account is fine, and it can be one you only use for this.

Google warns that the app isn't verified. Is that OK?

Until Google finishes reviewing Roster Sync, it may show "Google hasn't verified this app". Click Advanced, then Go to Roster Sync. Roster Sync can only see and change the Work roster calendar it creates, never your other calendars, email or files.

Does it work for my health district?

Yes, if your roster is on NSW Health's Employee Online. It works on every Employee Online address ending in .ros.health.nsw.gov.au, for example patheol, nnsweol, awheol and wnsweol. Work in two districts? Open each roster and both sets of shifts go into the same Work roster calendar without replacing each other.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer. Safari isn't supported. In Firefox, Google sometimes asks you to click Connect on the panel again; one click reconnects.

How do I stop it or remove it?

Click the Roster Sync button in your browser's toolbar and choose Disconnect, then remove the extension the way you'd remove any other. If you like, you can also delete the Work roster calendar. Your other calendars are never touched.

Does it cost anything?

No. Roster Sync is free.

Something not working?

Email hello@ducktake.com with what happened and which browser and calendar you use. Please leave out anything private, like other people's names from the roster.
