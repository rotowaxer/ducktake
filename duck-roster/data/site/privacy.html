Roster Sync

Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Roster Sync is a free browser extension by an individual developer at ducktake.com. Questions: hello@ducktake.com

  • What it reads: the shifts on your Employee Online roster page (sites ending in .ros.health.nsw.gov.au). Nothing else, and never your password.
  • Where they go: straight from your browser to a Work roster calendar it creates in your Google account. Not to us or anyone else.
  • Google access: it can only manage calendars it created, and reads the names in your calendar list to find its own. It never sees the events in your other calendars, your email or your files. Its sign-in token lasts about an hour and stays in your browser.
  • Stored on your computer: its calendar's ID, when it last synced, and your settings (automatic sync, colours, light or dark). No shift details.
  • Never: sold, shared, used for ads or tracked. No analytics.
  • Stop and delete: click Disconnect in the toolbar popup (or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions), delete the Work roster calendar, and remove the extension.
  • This website: no cookies or analytics. Cloudflare handles connection data such as IP addresses to deliver it.

Roster Sync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
